Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our services

How do I list my property?

Simply click on "List Property" and fill out the form with your property details. Our team will review and publish your listing within 24 hours.

Is there a fee for listing properties?

Basic listings are free. Premium listings with enhanced visibility and features are available for a small fee.

How do I contact property owners?

Click on "Contact Owner" on any property listing to send a message directly to the property owner through our secure messaging system.

Are all properties verified?

We verify all property listings through our team of local experts to ensure accuracy and authenticity of information provided.

Can I schedule property viewings?

Yes, you can schedule viewings directly through our platform. Property owners will receive your request and can confirm the appointment.

What areas do you cover?

We cover all major cities and towns across Bangladesh, including Dhaka, Chittagong, Sylhet, Rajshahi, Khulna, Barisal, Rangpur, and Mymensingh.
