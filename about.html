About CommonHouse

Connecting tenants with their perfect home across Bangladesh since 2020

Our Mission

CommonHouse is dedicated to revolutionizing the rental property market in Bangladesh by providing a seamless, transparent, and user-friendly platform that connects property owners with potential tenants.

We believe everyone deserves to find their perfect home, and every property owner deserves to find reliable tenants. Our comprehensive platform makes this process simple, efficient, and trustworthy.
